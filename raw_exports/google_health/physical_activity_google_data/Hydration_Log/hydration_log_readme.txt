Time Series Data Export

The Time Series export provides a detailed timeline of your tracked activity.

Files Included:
----------

hydration_log_YYYY-MM-DD.csv          - Where YYYY-MM-DD is the starting date for the entries in the file.

Logged hydration intake, representing the amount of liquid consumed.
Each entry has the following values:

    start time                        - Start date and time of the hydration log.
    end time                          - End date and time of the hydration log.
    amount consumed                   - The amount of liquid (ex. water) consumed.
    data source                       - The origin or source of this data.
