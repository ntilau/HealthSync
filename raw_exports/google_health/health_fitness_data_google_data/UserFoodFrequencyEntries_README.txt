UserFoodFrequencyEntries

The below section is dedicated to the exported data of the UserFoodFrequencyEntries data domain.
It contains data about the food frequency entries of the user.

Files Included:
----------

UserFoodFrequencyEntries.csv

The data for user food frequency entries
    AmountLastConsumed           - the amount of food last consumed
    DateLastEaten                - the date the food was last eaten
    FoodId                       - the unique identifier of the food
    LastEatenMap                 - auxiliary data regarding the last time the food was eaten
    Calories                     - the amount of calories in the food
    MeasurementUnitNameLastUsed  - the name of the measurement unit last used
    MeasurementUnitIdLastUsed    - the id of the measurement unit last used
    TimesConsumed                - how many times the food has been consumed
    MealType                     - the type of meal the food was consumed in
